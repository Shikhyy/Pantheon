Landing Page — Horizontal Parallax

Create 3D depth illusion by moving layers at different speeds on mouse/touch horizontal input

Parallax Layer Structure

🏛️ Background (Slowest)

Speed: 0.2x
Parthenon columns, dawn sky, distant mountains. Moves least.

🌫️ Midground (Medium)

Speed: 0.5x
Athena constellation, golden glow, atmospheric particles. Moderate movement.

✨ Foreground (Fastest)

Speed: 0.8x
Torch flames, dust motes, UI elements. Moves most with horizontal input.

How It Works

Interaction Model

Input: Mouse drag (desktop) or touch swipe (mobile) horizontally

Range: ±200px from center creates full parallax range

Vertical scroll: Still drives GSAP ScrollTrigger for story panels

3D Canvas: Camera doesn't move — layers within the 3D scene shift on X-axis

// Parallax calculation
const parallaxX = (mouseX / windowWidth - 0.5) * 200
bgLayer.position.x = parallaxX * 0.2
mgLayer.position.x = parallaxX * 0.5
fgLayer.position.x = parallaxX * 0.8

Visual Result

Mouse Left (–200px)
Background ← slow
🏛️ Columns shift left
Midground ← medium
🔥 Torches shift left (fast)
Creates sense of looking around the scene
Mouse Right (+200px)
Background → slow
🏛️ Columns shift right
Midground → medium
🔥 Torches shift right (fast)
Scene reveals different angles

Integration with Vertical Scroll

Both interactions work together:

  • Horizontal parallax: Mouse/touch creates 3D depth illusion (always active on landing)
  • Vertical scroll: ScrollTrigger reveals story panels (I → II → III → IV)
  • Navigation: Top nav bar stays fixed, works as normal
  • Exit: Scrolling past last panel or clicking nav triggers camera flythrough to next section
